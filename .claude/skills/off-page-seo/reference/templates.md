keywords (from `DATA_getDomainKeywords`), and the target location. Placeholders in
`{{curly}}`. Keep anchor text natural — see the anchor plan at the bottom.

## Anchor-text plan (keep it natural)
Build the anchor mix from the site's real keywords; avoid over-optimization.
Recommended distribution across a link campaign:
- **Branded** ({{Brand}}, {{Brand}}.com): ~40%
- **Naked URL** ({{url}}): ~20%
- **Generic** ("click here", "this site", "read more"): ~15%
- **Partial-match** ({{keyword}} + brand / long-tail): ~20%
- **Exact-match** (pure money keyword): ≤ 5% — use rarely; over-use triggers penalties.

Rotate anchors; never point dozens of exact-match commercial anchors at the same page.
